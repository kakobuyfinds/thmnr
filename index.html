The quick answer

What is a Kakobuy spreadsheet?

A Kakobuy spreadsheet is a shopping reference list used to organize product links and comparison notes for people researching purchases through Kakobuy. Its usefulness depends on the accuracy of its entries. Treat it as a starting point for research: check the original listing, measurements, seller information and applicable fees before purchasing.

Practical answers about the guide, product comparisons and shopping costs.

What is a Kakobuy spreadsheet?

A Kakobuy spreadsheet is a shopping reference list used to organize product links and comparison notes for people researching purchases through Kakobuy. The fields and quality of information vary by creator; a listing alone does not verify a seller, product authenticity or availability.

Is this an official Kakobuy website?

No. This is an independent shopping guide that links to CAFANS. It is not an official Kakobuy website and does not claim a partnership with Kakobuy or any featured brand.

Does this page provide a downloadable spreadsheet?

No. This page provides a category directory and comparison guide. Its shopping links open CAFANS; it does not provide a downloadable spreadsheet or live inventory feed.

How should I compare shoe and clothing sizes?

Compare the listing’s measurements with a similar item you already own. For shoes, review the seller’s foot-length or insole-length guidance and distinguish between those measurements. For clothing, compare chest, shoulder, length and waist measurements instead of relying only on a letter size.

Do QC photos prove authenticity?

No. Quality-check photos may help you inspect visible details, sizing labels, color and condition. They do not independently prove authenticity, material composition, durability or seller authorization.

Is the listed product price the final cost?

Not necessarily. Depending on the transaction, the total may include domestic delivery, service fees, international shipping, optional services, taxes and duties. Check the applicable charges and current terms before paying.
